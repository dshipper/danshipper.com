Dan Shipper

I'm the co-founder and CEO of Every, a media and software company exploring what AI means for how we work and think. I write Chain of Thought, a weekly column about AI read by more than 100,000 people, and host AI & I, a podcast about how the smartest people in the world use AI.

Before Every, I co-founded Firefly, a customer-support software company I started while a student at Penn and sold to Pegasystems in 2014. I've been writing online since 2010. I live in New York City.

Now

Every is a living laboratory for the future of work: a small team where AI does much of the heavy lifting. We publish a daily newsletter read by 100,000+ builders, and we make software—Cora (email), Sparkle (file organization), Spiral (writing), Monologue (dictation), Proof (collaborative editing), and Plus One (an AI agent in Slack). We incubate new products in Every Studio and train teams at other companies to work the way we do.

The through-line in my work: AI is less interesting as a tool than as a mirror—it changes what we make, and it changes who we are while we make it.

Questions people ask

Who is Dan Shipper?

The co-founder and CEO of Every, a media and software company in New York City. I write Chain of Thought, a weekly column about AI read by more than 100,000 people, and host the podcast AI & I. Before Every, I co-founded Firefly, which was acquired by Pegasystems in 2014.

What is Every?

Every is a daily newsletter and software studio about AI and the future of work, read by 100,000+ founders, operators, and investors. We build Cora, Sparkle, Spiral, Monologue, Proof, and Plus One; incubate products in Every Studio; and run an AI training and consulting practice.

What is the allocation economy?

A term I coined in a January 2024 essay: the shift from being paid for what you know to being paid for how well you allocate intelligence—as AI turns every maker into a manager of models.

What is AI & I?

My interview podcast about how the smartest people in the world use AI—116 episodes and counting since 2023. Guests have included Kevin Roose, Governor Phil Murphy, and some of Silicon Valley's best investors. Apple · Spotify · YouTube.
